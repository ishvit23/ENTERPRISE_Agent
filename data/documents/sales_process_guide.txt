SALES DEPARTMENT - SALES PROCESS AND METHODOLOGY
=================================================

SALES STAGES
------------
1. PROSPECTING (0%)
   - Identify potential customers
   - Research company and contacts
   - Initial outreach via email/call/LinkedIn
   - Qualify basic fit

2. DISCOVERY (10%)
   - Initial meeting scheduled
   - Understand business challenges
   - Identify decision makers
   - Document requirements using MEDDIC

3. QUALIFICATION (25%)
   - BANT confirmed (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
   - Technical requirements gathered
   - Competition identified
   - Champion identified

4. DEMO/EVALUATION (50%)
   - Product demonstration completed
   - POC/Trial initiated if applicable
   - Technical validation
   - Business case developed

5. PROPOSAL (75%)
   - Pricing proposal delivered
   - Contract terms discussed
   - Procurement process understood
   - Executive sponsor engaged

6. NEGOTIATION (90%)
   - Terms finalized
   - Legal review in progress
   - Final approvals pending
   - Verbal commitment received

7. CLOSED WON (100%)
   - Contract signed
   - PO received
   - Handoff to Customer Success

MEDDIC FRAMEWORK
----------------
M - Metrics: What business outcomes will customer achieve?
E - Economic Buyer: Who has budget authority?
D - Decision Criteria: How will they evaluate solutions?
D - Decision Process: What steps to make a purchase?
I - Identify Pain: What problems are they solving?
C - Champion: Who will advocate internally for us?

DEAL DESK PROCESS
-----------------
Submit to Deal Desk for:
- Discounts over 15%
- Non-standard payment terms
- Custom contract clauses
- Multi-year agreements
- Enterprise deals over $100K

SLA for Deal Desk response: 24 hours

QUOTING AND PRICING
-------------------
Standard Products:
- Use CPQ (Configure Price Quote) tool
- List prices in Salesforce Price Book
- Discount approval matrix applies

Custom Solutions:
- Engage Solutions Engineering
- Custom SOW required
- Finance approval for non-standard terms

SALESFORCE HYGIENE
------------------
Required Fields:
- Close Date (realistic, updated weekly)
- Amount (committed value)
- Next Steps (always current)
- Stage (accurate to reality)
- MEDDIC fields populated

Updates required:
- Before every forecast call
- After every customer interaction
- When deal dynamics change

FORECAST CATEGORIES
-------------------
- Commit: 90%+ confidence, closing this period
- Best Case: 60-89% confidence
- Pipeline: 25-59% confidence
- Upside: Under 25% confidence

TERRITORY RULES
---------------
- Named accounts assigned to AEs
- Geographic territories for SMB
- Industry verticals for Enterprise
- Round-robin for inbound leads

COMMISSION STRUCTURE
--------------------
Base + Variable compensation
On-target earnings (OTE) = Base + 100% target commission
Accelerators kick in at 100% of quota
Quarterly MBOs available for strategic initiatives
