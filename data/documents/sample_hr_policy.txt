HR Leave Policy:
All employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year. To apply for leave, employees must submit a leave application form to the HR department at least 2 weeks in advance. Emergency leave can be requested by contacting HR directly.

Other guidelines:
- Maintain confidentiality of company information.
- Follow the official leave application process.
- Report any workplace issues to HR immediately.
