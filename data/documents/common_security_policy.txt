CLOUD9 ENTERPRISE - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
This policy applies to ALL employees, contractors, and vendors with access to Cloud9 systems.

PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
---------------------
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include: uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Change passwords every 90 days
- No password reuse for last 10 passwords
- Never share passwords with anyone

MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
---------------------------------
MFA is MANDATORY for:
- All cloud applications
- VPN access
- Email access from personal devices
- Administrative consoles

DATA CLASSIFICATION
-------------------
1. PUBLIC - Can be shared externally (marketing materials, press releases)
2. INTERNAL - For employees only (internal memos, org charts)
3. CONFIDENTIAL - Sensitive business data (financial reports, strategies)
4. RESTRICTED - Highly sensitive (PII, health data, trade secrets)

DATA HANDLING
-------------
- Never store CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data on personal devices
- Use only approved cloud storage (OneDrive, approved SharePoint sites)
- Encrypt all sensitive files before sharing
- Shred physical documents containing sensitive information

PHISHING & SOCIAL ENGINEERING
-----------------------------
- Verify sender email addresses carefully
- Never click suspicious links
- Report phishing attempts to security@cloud9.com
- IT will NEVER ask for your password via email or phone

DEVICE SECURITY
---------------
- Lock your computer when away (Win+L or Cmd+Ctrl+Q)
- Enable full disk encryption
- Install updates promptly when notified
- Report lost or stolen devices IMMEDIATELY to IT

INCIDENT REPORTING
------------------
Report security incidents to: security@cloud9.com
Emergency Security Hotline: ext. 9999
All incidents must be reported within 24 hours.

CONSEQUENCES
------------
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
