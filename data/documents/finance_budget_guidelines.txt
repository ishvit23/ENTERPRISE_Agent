FINANCE DEPARTMENT - BUDGET PLANNING GUIDELINES
================================================

FISCAL YEAR CALENDAR
--------------------
Fiscal Year: January 1 - December 31
Q1: January - March
Q2: April - June
Q3: July - September
Q4: October - December

ANNUAL BUDGET CYCLE
-------------------
August: Budget kickoff and templates distributed
September: Department heads submit initial budgets
October: Finance review and consolidation
November: Executive review and adjustments
December: Final approval by Board
January 1: New fiscal year begins

BUDGET CATEGORIES
-----------------

1. Personnel Costs (typically 60-70% of budget)
   - Salaries and wages
   - Bonuses and commissions
   - Benefits (health, dental, vision)
   - Payroll taxes
   - Contractor costs
   - Training and development

2. Operating Expenses
   - Office rent and utilities
   - Software subscriptions
   - Hardware and equipment
   - Office supplies
   - Telecommunications
   - Professional services

3. Travel and Entertainment
   - Business travel
   - Client entertainment
   - Team events
   - Conference attendance

4. Marketing and Sales
   - Advertising and campaigns
   - Events and sponsorships
   - Collateral and content
   - Lead generation tools

5. Capital Expenditures (CapEx)
   - Major equipment purchases
   - Infrastructure investments
   - Building improvements

BUDGET VARIANCE REPORTING
-------------------------
Monthly variance reports due by 10th of following month.
Variances over 10% require written explanation.
Corrective action plans needed for significant overruns.

REFORECASTING
-------------
Quarterly reforecasts required:
- Q1 reforecast: April 15
- Q2 reforecast: July 15
- Q3 reforecast: October 15

COST CENTER STRUCTURE
---------------------
Format: DEPT-TYPE-PROJECT
Example: ENG-OPEX-PLATFORM

Departments:
- ENG: Engineering
- SAL: Sales
- MKT: Marketing
- FIN: Finance
- HR: Human Resources
- IT: Information Technology
- OPS: Operations
- LEG: Legal

Types:
- OPEX: Operating expenses
- CAPEX: Capital expenditures
- PERS: Personnel costs

APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
-------------------
Unbudgeted expenses require approval:
- Under $5,000: Department Head
- $5,000 - $25,000: VP
- $25,000 - $100,000: CFO
- Over $100,000: CEO + CFO

HEADCOUNT PLANNING
------------------
All new positions require:
1. Job description and justification
2. Budget allocation identified
3. HR and Finance sign-off
4. VP or C-level approval
