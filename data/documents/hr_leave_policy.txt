HR DEPARTMENT - LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY
==========================================

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
-------------------
Annual PTO Allocation:
- 0-2 years tenure: 15 days
- 3-5 years tenure: 20 days
- 6-10 years tenure: 25 days
- 10+ years tenure: 30 days

PTO accrues monthly and can be carried over (max 5 days to next year).
Unused PTO beyond carryover limit will be forfeited on December 31.

SICK LEAVE
----------
- 10 paid sick days per year
- Doctor's note required for absences > 3 consecutive days
- Sick leave cannot be converted to PTO or paid out

PARENTAL LEAVE
--------------
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid leave
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks paid leave
- Adoption/Foster: Same benefits as birth parents
- Must notify HR at least 30 days in advance when possible

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
-----------------
- Immediate family: 5 days paid
- Extended family: 3 days paid
- Close friend: 1 day paid

JURY DUTY
---------
Full pay for duration of jury service. Provide court documentation to HR.

REQUESTING TIME OFF
-------------------
1. Submit request in Workday at least 2 weeks in advance
2. Manager approval required
3. Peak blackout periods may apply (check with your department)
4. Emergency leave: Contact HR directly

LEAVE OF ABSENCE (UNPAID)
-------------------------
- Personal leave up to 30 days may be granted
- Requires VP approval
- Benefits continue during approved leave
- Job protection guaranteed for approved leaves
