LEGAL DEPARTMENT - CONTRACT AND AGREEMENT POLICY
=================================================

AUTHORITY TO SIGN CONTRACTS
---------------------------
Only authorized personnel may sign contracts on behalf of Cloud9:
- CEO: Unlimited authority
- CFO: Up to $5M
- VPs: Up to $500K (within their department)
- Directors: Up to $100K (within their department)
- Managers: Up to $25K (standard terms only)

All contracts MUST be reviewed by Legal before signature.

CONTRACT TYPES
--------------

1. Customer Agreements
   - Master Service Agreement (MSA)
   - Order Forms/SOWs
   - NDAs
   - DPAs (Data Processing Agreements)

2. Vendor Agreements
   - Software licenses
   - SaaS subscriptions
   - Professional services
   - Procurement contracts

3. Partnership Agreements
   - Channel partner agreements
   - Technology partnerships
   - Referral agreements

4. Employment/HR Agreements
   - Offer letters
   - Employment agreements
   - Separation agreements
   - Contractor agreements

CONTRACT REVIEW PROCESS
-----------------------
1. Requester submits contract to Legal via Jira LEGAL project
2. Include: Contract document, business justification, deadline
3. Legal reviews within SLA:
   - Standard contracts: 3 business days
   - Complex contracts: 5-10 business days
   - Expedited: 24-48 hours (VP approval required)
4. Legal provides redlines/comments
5. Negotiation as needed
6. Final approval and signature

NON-NEGOTIABLE TERMS
--------------------
The following terms cannot be modified without CEO approval:
- Unlimited liability
- Indemnification caps below insurance coverage
- Audit rights granting unrestricted access
- IP ownership changes
- Exclusivity clauses
- Non-compete beyond standard employment terms
- Governing law outside US/UK/EU

STANDARD TERMS
--------------
Our standard positions:
- Liability: Capped at 12 months of fees paid
- Payment terms: Net 30
- Contract term: Annual with auto-renewal
- Termination: 30 days notice
- Governing law: Delaware, USA
- Dispute resolution: Binding arbitration

NDA PROCESS
-----------
Standard Mutual NDA:
- Use Cloud9 template (on Legal SharePoint)
- Self-service for standard terms
- 2-year confidentiality period
- Mutual obligations

Non-standard NDA:
- Submit to Legal for review
- Common issues: one-way, extended terms, broad definitions

DATA PROTECTION
---------------
All agreements involving personal data must include:
- GDPR-compliant DPA (EU customers)
- CCPA addendum (California customers)
- Standard Contractual Clauses (international transfers)

Submit DPA requests with main contract.

RECORD KEEPING
--------------
All signed contracts must be:
- Uploaded to Contract Management System (Ironclad)
- Tagged with correct metadata
- Linked to Salesforce opportunity (customer contracts)
- Stored for duration of contract + 7 years
