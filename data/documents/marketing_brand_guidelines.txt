MARKETING DEPARTMENT - BRAND GUIDELINES
========================================

BRAND IDENTITY
--------------
Company Name: Cloud9 Enterprise
Tagline: "Elevate Your Business"
Brand Voice: Professional, innovative, approachable, trustworthy

LOGO USAGE
----------
Primary Logo:
- Full color on white/light backgrounds
- White version on dark backgrounds
- Minimum size: 120px width digital, 1" print
- Clear space: Height of the "9" on all sides

Logo Don'ts:
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't change colors
- Don't add effects (shadows, gradients)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds
- Don't rotate or flip

COLOR PALETTE
-------------
Primary Colors:
- Cloud Blue: #667eea (primary brand color)
- Deep Purple: #764ba2 (accent color)
- Midnight: #1a1a2e (text/dark backgrounds)

Secondary Colors:
- Sky: #a8d8ea
- Mist: #f0f4f8
- Storm: #4a5568

Accent Colors:
- Success Green: #48bb78
- Warning Yellow: #ecc94b
- Error Red: #f56565

TYPOGRAPHY
----------
Primary Font: Inter
- Headlines: Inter Bold (700)
- Subheadlines: Inter Semibold (600)
- Body: Inter Regular (400)
- Captions: Inter Light (300)

Secondary Font: Source Code Pro (for code/technical content)

PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
-----------------
- Authentic, diverse representation
- Natural lighting preferred
- Modern office and technology settings
- Avoid stock photo clichés
- High resolution (minimum 300dpi print, 72dpi web)

IMAGE GUIDELINES
----------------
- Hero images: 16:9 aspect ratio
- Blog thumbnails: 1200x630px
- Social media: Platform-specific sizes
- All images must be properly licensed

MESSAGING PILLARS
-----------------
1. Innovation: Leading-edge technology solutions
2. Reliability: Enterprise-grade performance and security
3. Partnership: We succeed when our customers succeed
4. Simplicity: Complex problems, elegant solutions

CONTENT GUIDELINES
------------------
Writing Style:
- Active voice preferred
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Bulleted lists for scanability
- Headlines that convey value
- Avoid jargon, explain technical terms

Tone by Channel:
- Website: Professional, confident
- Blog: Educational, helpful
- Social: Conversational, engaging
- Email: Personalized, action-oriented
- Sales Materials: Solution-focused, ROI-driven
