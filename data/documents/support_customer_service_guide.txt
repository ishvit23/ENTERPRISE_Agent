SUPPORT DEPARTMENT - CUSTOMER SERVICE HANDBOOK
===============================================

SUPPORT MISSION
---------------
"Deliver exceptional support that turns customers into advocates."

SUPPORT TIERS
-------------
Tier 1 - Frontline Support
- First point of contact
- Basic troubleshooting
- Account and billing questions
- Known issue resolution
- Escalate complex issues to Tier 2

Tier 2 - Technical Support
- Advanced troubleshooting
- Product configuration
- Integration support
- Bug reproduction and documentation
- Escalate to Engineering when needed

Tier 3 - Engineering Support
- Code-level debugging
- Custom solutions
- Critical bug fixes
- Architecture guidance

SUPPORT CHANNELS
----------------
- Email: support@cloud9.com
- Phone: 1-800-CLOUD9-1
- Chat: In-app and website
- Portal: support.cloud9.com

Hours by Plan:
- Basic: M-F 9AM-6PM EST
- Professional: M-F 8AM-8PM EST
- Enterprise: 24/7/365

SLA RESPONSE TIMES
------------------
                   Basic    Professional   Enterprise
Critical (P1):     8 hrs    4 hrs          1 hr
High (P2):         24 hrs   8 hrs          4 hrs
Medium (P3):       48 hrs   24 hrs         8 hrs
Low (P4):          72 hrs   48 hrs         24 hrs

Resolution targets are 4x response times.

PRIORITY DEFINITIONS
--------------------
P1 - Critical: Production down, no workaround, affecting business
P2 - High: Major feature broken, workaround exists
P3 - Medium: Feature issue, minimal business impact
P4 - Low: Questions, feature requests, minor issues

TICKET WORKFLOW
---------------
1. New: Ticket created
2. Open: Agent assigned and working
3. Pending: Waiting for customer response
4. On Hold: Waiting for internal team (Engineering, PM)
5. Solved: Resolution provided
6. Closed: Customer confirmed or 7 days since solved

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
----------------------
First Response:
- Acknowledge receipt
- Set expectations
- Ask clarifying questions if needed
- Provide immediate help if possible

Updates:
- Update every 24 hours minimum
- Proactively communicate delays
- Summarize progress and next steps

Resolution:
- Clearly explain solution
- Provide documentation links
- Offer additional assistance
- Request feedback

ESCALATION PROCESS
------------------
Customer request → Tier 1 Manager
Technical issues → Tier 2
Product bugs → Engineering via Jira
Account issues → Customer Success Manager
Executive escalation → Support Director

KNOWLEDGE BASE
--------------
Always check KB before answering:
- docs.cloud9.com
- Internal KB (Confluence)
- Past tickets in Zendesk

Update KB when:
- New issue patterns emerge
- Workarounds discovered
- New features released

CSAT AND QUALITY
----------------
Target CSAT: 95%
Surveys sent after ticket closure.
Quality reviews: 5 tickets per agent per week
Coaching sessions: Weekly 1:1s with team lead

TOOLS
-----
- Zendesk: Ticketing system
- Intercom: Chat support
- Zoom: Screen sharing
- Jira: Bug tracking
- Confluence: Knowledge base
- Salesforce: Customer information

PROHIBITED ACTIONS
------------------
Never:
- Share customer data externally
- Make promises about unreleased features
- Provide refunds without manager approval
- Access customer accounts without permission
- Blame other teams in customer communication
