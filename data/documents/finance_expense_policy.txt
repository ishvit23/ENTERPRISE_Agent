FINANCE DEPARTMENT - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
==================================================

GENERAL GUIDELINES
------------------
All expenses must be:
- Business-related and necessary
- Pre-approved for amounts over $100
- Submitted within 30 days of incurrence
- Supported by valid receipts

EXPENSE CATEGORIES AND LIMITS
-----------------------------

Travel - Airfare:
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours
- Business class for flights over 6 hours (VP approval required)
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible
- Use corporate travel portal (Concur)

Travel - Hotels:
- Maximum $250/night domestic
- Maximum $350/night international
- Maximum $400/night in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, London)
- Use preferred hotel partners for discounts

Travel - Ground Transportation:
- Rental cars: Compact or midsize only
- Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): Allowed for business travel
- Personal vehicle: $0.67/mile reimbursement
- Parking: Actual cost with receipt

Meals - While Traveling:
- Breakfast: $20 maximum
- Lunch: $30 maximum
- Dinner: $50 maximum
- Alcohol not reimbursable unless client entertainment

Client Entertainment:
- Pre-approval required for expenses over $200
- Maximum $150/person for meals
- Document attendees and business purpose
- VP approval for events over $1,000

Office Supplies:
- Use approved vendors (Staples, Amazon Business)
- Orders over $500 require manager approval
- IT equipment must go through IT procurement

APPROVAL MATRIX
---------------
$0 - $100: Self-approved (with receipt)
$101 - $500: Manager approval
$501 - $2,000: Director approval
$2,001 - $10,000: VP approval
$10,000+: CFO approval

REIMBURSEMENT PROCESS
---------------------
1. Log into Concur expense system
2. Create expense report
3. Attach all receipts (photos acceptable)
4. Add business justification
5. Submit for approval
6. Reimbursement within 10 business days of approval

NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
-------------------------
- Personal travel extensions
- Gym memberships while traveling
- In-room movies or minibar
- Traffic or parking violations
- Personal phone calls
- Upgrades without approval
- Expenses without receipts over $25

CORPORATE CREDIT CARDS
----------------------
- Available for employees with frequent travel
- Must be used for business expenses only
- Statements reconciled monthly
- Report lost/stolen cards immediately to Finance
