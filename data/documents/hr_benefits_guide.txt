HR DEPARTMENT - EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE 2026
=============================================

HEALTH INSURANCE
----------------
Three plan options (employee contribution per month):
1. Basic Plan: $150/month - 80% coverage, $2000 deductible
2. Standard Plan: $250/month - 90% coverage, $1000 deductible
3. Premium Plan: $400/month - 95% coverage, $500 deductible

Family coverage available (+$300-600/month depending on plan).
Open enrollment: November 1-30 each year.

DENTAL INSURANCE
----------------
- Preventive care: 100% covered
- Basic procedures: 80% covered
- Major procedures: 50% covered
- Annual maximum: $2,000 per person
- Cost: $30/month individual, $75/month family

VISION INSURANCE
----------------
- Annual eye exam: $10 copay
- Frames allowance: $200/year
- Contact lens allowance: $150/year
- Cost: $15/month individual, $35/month family

401(K) RETIREMENT
-----------------
- Company matches 100% up to 6% of salary
- Immediate vesting
- Contribution limit: $23,000/year (2026)
- Catch-up contribution (50+): Additional $7,500

LIFE INSURANCE
--------------
- Basic: 1x salary (company paid)
- Supplemental: Up to 5x salary available (employee paid)
- AD&D included with all life insurance plans

DISABILITY INSURANCE
--------------------
- Short-term: 60% of salary for up to 26 weeks (company paid)
- Long-term: 60% of salary after 26 weeks (company paid)

WELLNESS PROGRAM
----------------
- Gym membership reimbursement: Up to $50/month
- Annual wellness bonus: $200 for completing health assessment
- Mental health support: 10 free therapy sessions/year via EAP
- Wellness challenges with prizes throughout the year

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
------------------------
- Tuition reimbursement: Up to $5,000/year
- Conference attendance: Budget varies by department
- LinkedIn Learning: Free access for all employees
- Internal training programs: Various options available

COMMUTER BENEFITS
-----------------
- Pre-tax transit/parking: Up to $300/month
- Bike-to-work reimbursement: $50/month
- EV charging: Free at company parking lots

EMPLOYEE DISCOUNTS
------------------
- Dell/Apple products: 15% off
- AT&T/Verizon wireless: 20% off
- Local gym partnerships: 25-40% off
- Various retail and entertainment discounts via Perks portal
